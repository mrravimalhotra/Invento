The whole app on one page

Each item category has its own lane. Approved raw material feeds production. Packaging stock feeds packing. Approved finished product is packed and then issued.

1. Item masters: three kinds of item

An item belongs to one of three categories. Raw material and Packaging items are added in Item Master. A Finished Product item is never added by hand: it appears when an MFR is approved.

Two different words, both called type. The category (Raw material, Packaging, Finished product) decides how the item behaves. The Item Type is a separate list kept in Item Type Master. A raw material cannot be saved without one. The COA template no longer depends on it: since 3 Oct 2026 each raw item and each MFR has its own.

2. Purchasing, and the three samples taken from raw material

A purchase order stays a draft until Final submit. Only active vendors can be chosen. The invoice date and the Received on date cannot be in the future, and they (and, while there are no lines, the vendor) can be corrected with Edit invoice details until Final submit. Price and GST are required on every line. For a raw material line, the QC, Stability and R&D sample quantities are entered on the line and leave stock at Final submit. A packaging line has no sample fields and no QC.

Reopening and limits. A System Admin can reopen a submitted order, which reverses its stock, but only until QC has a record on any of its batches, and never once a batch was used, written off or rejected. Quantities, prices and text fields have sensible upper limits; a figure ten times larger than the last purchase of that item asks for a tick before it is saved.

3. Raw material QC: using the QC sample

QC is recorded against the QC sample already set aside at purchase, so saving a QC record moves no stock. Each record gets an Analytical Report No. (ARRM-0001/26 style). Two rounds, by two different people, are needed before a batch is usable. The Reviewer sets the expiry date and the number of days until retest.

Results, reasons and rejection. Round 1 (Quality Checker) types the result of every test on the COA template and gives a written reason (at least 3 characters) for Approve or Reject. A Round 1 rejection is only a proposal: the batch stays in stock until Round 2 (QC Reviewer) confirms it or sends it back with a reason. The Reviewer may edit the results; if so, the Reviewer’s results are final and go on the Certificate of Analysis, otherwise Round 1’s stand. Both sets are kept, with a decision history. A rejected record can be voided by a QC Reviewer or System Admin with a reason; the stock returns. A raw material with no COA template cannot start Round 1.
Retest limits. A raw material batch is retested at most three times, each retest period at most 180 days (pre-filled with 180). On the third retest the Reviewer sets only the expiry date. A batch past its expiry date is never retested. Rejected raw material leaves On hand at once and shows under Rejected Materials. If two people decide the same record together, the first decision is saved and the second is told to refresh.

4. Certificate of Analysis

A COA is generated for an approved QC record, raw material or finished product, from the template of that raw item or MFR. Results are entered per test and the certificate is saved as a snapshot.

Who edits a template follows the item or MFR: for a raw material, System Admin, Inventory Manager and MFR Manager; for an MFR, System Admin and MFR Manager. QC roles issue certificates but no longer edit templates.

5. Finished product: recipe, batch, QC

The MFR is approved once and creates the finished-product item. Each batch is composed automatically from approved raw material, oldest stock first (nobody picks batches), and the raw material is held as soon as the Draft is saved. A Draft that is not started within 30 minutes cancels itself and releases the stock. The batch is then completed and cleared by the same two-round QC using the finished-product QC sample.

Completing a batch. The yield is shown as a percentage of the batch size and the range the MFR allows; outside that range a reason is required, and above 150% it is refused as a likely typing mistake. A review step comes before saving, because a completed batch cannot be corrected on screen. Batch numbers come in two forms: the full FP-00001-01/26 and a short PR-01/26 (Domestic) or OR-01/26 (Export) that printed documents and Reports use. A completed batch page shows samples taken, bulk used by packaging, bulk left and each packing run.
Retest of a finished batch. A finished product batch follows the same cycle as raw material: when its retest date arrives it appears on the QC page under Finished product due for retest, and Start Retest takes the sample from the stability sample first, then from the bulk. At most three retests, each at most 180 days; the third takes only an expiry date; a retest never extends the expiry and its date can never fall after it. While a retest is open, or once the batch is expired or past its retest date, packing and finished product labels are refused. A rejected retest takes the bulk out of On hand (packs stay); voiding it brings the bulk back. The batch page lists First QC and each retest.

6. Packaging and issue, and product returning as raw material

Only approved batches can be packed. Every issue is a pack: there is no repack or unpack. One save can hold up to 30 lines (different pack sizes or batches), each with its own packaging materials and its own PKG- code, and it is all saved or none. The packed product is issued to Store, R&D, or Production, where a Production issue becomes a new raw material.

7. Opening stock: loading what is already on the shelves

Before go-live the physical stock count is loaded at Admin → Opening Stock, either from an Excel sheet or by typing the rows in on the page; both go through the same checks. Raw material, packaging and finished product (packed and bulk) each have their own template and their own typed-in form. Loaded batches are tagged Legacy wherever they appear, and old batch and AR numbers are kept as typed.

Units and numbers. Raw material and packaging rows carry a Unit column; if it differs from the item’s own unit but is the same kind (g for kg) the quantity is converted, otherwise the row is refused. Batch numbers and AR numbers that look like ones the app makes (ITEM-0001/26, ARRM-0001/26, ARFP-0001/26) are refused so an old number can never be mistaken for a new one.

8. Seeing stock: the Inventory Ledger tabs and the integrity check

Every stock event writes one row to the inventory ledger. Eight tabs on the Inventory Ledger page read it in different ways, and a System Admin check proves the ledger and the batches still agree.

Rules the database enforces

Approved and in date onlyNo screen or direct call can consume a raw material batch that is not QC-approved or whose retest date has passed. Packing and labelling a finished product batch is refused when it is expired, past its retest date or being retested.
No negative stockWastage, samples, composition and packaging are refused when the batch or item does not have the quantity.
Oldest stock firstA finished-product batch must use the oldest approved batch of an ingredient before a newer one.
Every change is loggedAll business tables write to the Audit Log, and at least one System Admin must always remain. Sign-ins, sign-outs, failed sign-in attempts and password changes are logged too.
Sensible limitsQuantities, prices and text lengths above fixed limits are refused, on screen and in the database, with a message that names the field.
No dates in the futureInvoice, received, batch start, finish and packing issue dates cannot be later than today; an expiry date cannot be in the past.
Someone else changed thisIf a record was changed by another person after you opened it, your save is refused and you are asked to refresh, so nobody overwrites a colleague unknowingly.
One person, one QC roundThe same person cannot approve both rounds on one record (System Admin is the only exception), and a decided record is final.
Retest and expiry limitsRaw material and finished product: at most three retests, each at most 180 days; a retest never extends the expiry and its date can never fall after it; an expired batch is never retested and never used.

Running alongside every step

Inventory LedgerEvery stock event (receipt, sample, consumption, wastage, issue, opening load) with a running balance per item and batch, in eight tabs including Stock Statement, Rejected Materials and Batch Trace.
DashboardLow stock, retest due within 90 days (raw material and finished product), finished-product expiry within 90 days, and finished product batches in progress.
ReportsRM Stock, QC Register, FP Register and Purchase Register, each with PDF and Excel export.
Audit LogWho changed what and when, plus sign-ins and failed attempts. Read by System Admin and Super Auditor.
Stock Integrity CheckSystem Admin screen that compares the ledger with the batches (section 8).
User GuideOverview → User Guide holds the guide and this process map, both as PDF.
AccessSix fixed roles (System Admin, Inventory Manager, MFR Manager, QC Checker, QC Reviewer, Super Auditor), enforced in the database.
